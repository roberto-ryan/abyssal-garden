# Assets

Everything in this folder is made by `source/assets.mjs` (`npm run assets` in `source/`) from the sources below.
All of them are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
The converter turns, scales and thins out the meshes; the textures are the originals.

| Files | In the game | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `leaper*` | Leaper | *Limulus polyphemus* (Atlantic horseshoe crab), 3D scan, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Invertebrate Zoology. [Record](http://n2t.net/ark:/65665/3faf99bfb-092d-41e8-b9f1-404ea6f35f69) |
| `spitter*` | Spitter | *Diodon hystrix* (spot-fin porcupinefish), USNM 195928, 3D scan, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Division of Fishes. [Record](http://n2t.net/ark:/65665/3f878a823-d6e9-4282-ad96-57e448c7f6ec) |
| `stalker*` | Stalker | *Grapsus planifrons* (shore crab), 3D scan, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Invertebrate Zoology. [Record](http://n2t.net/ark:/65665/3498a25b8-2d98-44bb-bf02-dd4871cdc7b0) |
| `caster*` | Caster | *Pycnopodia helianthoides* (sunflower sea star), 3D scan, Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Invertebrate Zoology. [Record](http://n2t.net/ark:/65665/397c8cc0d-359e-4f3c-99a8-eccd218b60cb) |
| `rifle*` | Rifle | [Bolt Action Rifle 7.62](https://polyhaven.com/a/bolt_action_rifle_7_62) by Mateusz Sadek, Poly Haven |

The scans come from [Smithsonian Open Access](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) through [3d.si.edu](https://3d.si.edu).
CC0 asks for no credit; it is given here anyway.
